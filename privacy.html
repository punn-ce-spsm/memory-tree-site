Memory Tree Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 October 2026

Summary

Memory Tree is a private journal. Your memories stay on your iPhone and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud private database. The developer has no server and cannot see your memories. The app has no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

What the app stores

  • The text of each memory you write.
  • An optional single photo for a memory.
  • The date of each memory, added automatically.
  • A setting that remembers you finished onboarding.
  • Your reminder schedule, if you turn the reminder on.

Where it is stored

Memories are stored on your device. They are synced only to your own iCloud private database through CloudKit, using your Apple ID. This sync is handled by Apple. The developer cannot access your iCloud private database.

What the developer can access

Nothing. The developer runs no server and receives no data from the app.

Photos and metadata

You choose a photo with the system photo picker. The app does not get access to your whole photo library. Before saving, the app downscales and re-encodes the photo. This removes location and camera metadata.

Notifications

The weekly reminder is optional. It is a local notification scheduled on your device. The default time is Sunday at 21:00. The reminder contains no memory content. You can change or turn it off at any time.

No tracking, analytics or third parties

The app has no analytics, no ads, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. The app collects no data about you.

Children

Memory Tree is not directed at children under 13. The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Your choices and deletion

  • Delete a single memory, or all memories, inside the app.
  • Delete the app to remove the data stored on your device.
  • To remove iCloud data, go to Settings > Apple ID > iCloud > Manage Storage, or delete all memories in the app.
  • Turn the reminder off in the app or in iOS Settings.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
