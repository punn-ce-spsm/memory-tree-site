Memory Tree Support

A calm place to keep your memories.

Memory Tree is a personal journal. Each memory you write becomes a blossom on a sakura tree. Shake your phone and one blossom falls, so you can read that memory again.

Where are my memories stored?

On your iPhone, and synced to your own iCloud private database. The developer has no server and cannot see them.

How do I delete a memory or all data?

Open a memory and delete it, or delete all memories in the app. Deleting the app removes the data on your device. You can also remove iCloud data in Settings > Apple ID > iCloud > Manage Storage.

How do I change or stop the reminder?

The weekly reminder is optional. Change the day and time, or turn it off, in the app. You can also turn off notifications in iOS Settings.
